OUR STORY

Built on the ground we work in.

Shelta Homes & Properties Ltd started with a simple frustration: too many property deals in Akwa Ibom fell apart after money had already changed hands. We set out to be the consultancy that catches problems before they cost anyone anything.

WHO WE ARE

A consultancy first, an agency second.

Based at 9 Udo Idung, off Nwaniba Road, we work across Uyo and the wider Akwa Ibom market — from first-time land buyers to landlords who need a property managed properly while they're elsewhere.

Most agents make their money the moment a deal closes. We built our business around the parts that come after: the lease that still needs enforcing eight months in, the tenant who needs referencing properly, the title document that needs a second look before anyone signs.

That's why our services span the full life of a property — buying and selling, leasing and management, development and relocation — rather than stopping at the handshake.

If you're new to Uyo, buying your first plot, or handing us a portfolio to manage, the same team stays with you from the first phone call onward.

WHAT WE HOLD OURSELVES TO

Four things we don't compromise on.

These aren't slogans on a wall — they're what we check against before any deal moves forward.

Verified before promised

We don't market a plot or property until its title and documentation have been checked. If something doesn't add up, we say so before you spend a naira.

Plain language, always

Lease terms, survey plans and contracts are walked through in language you'd use with a friend, not legal shorthand designed to be skimmed.

Local, and staying local

We work Uyo's streets ourselves. Advice on an area comes from having stood on it, not from a listing sheet.

Responsible after the sale

Management and leasing clients hear from us on a schedule, not only when something has gone wrong.

WHERE WE OPERATE

Rooted on Nwaniba Road, working across Akwa Ibom.

Our office sits off the four-lane by the water fountain — close enough to Uyo's growth corridors to walk most of the plots we list. Coverage extends further as clients need it.
